What happens when
half the system succeeds?

My work centres on batch processing. This project pushed me to ask what happens when one component succeeds, another fails, and the caller cannot tell the difference.

As the system grew, so did the ways it could fail. The question became: what has already happened, what can safely be retried, and how would I know?

    The shift in my thinking

    More components. More failure combinations.

    I began to see engineering as reasoning about failures between components as much as behaviour within them. A committed transaction and a failed response can coexist. A delivered event and an unacknowledged offset can coexist. Recovery starts by distinguishing those states.

    The next step is to turn these questions into repeatable fault tests. This map makes the claims and the gaps visible—including where a local mock or an architectural proposal stops short of a demonstrated guarantee.
